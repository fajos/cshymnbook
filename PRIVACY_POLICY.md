## 1. Introduction

Welcome to **C&S Hymn Book**, developed by **FajosTech** ("we", "our", or "us"). We are deeply committed to respecting and protecting the privacy of our users. 

This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles user information. **In short: We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any of your personal data.** The app is designed for spiritual devotion and church worship and operates completely offline.

---

## 2. Information We Do Not Collect

- **No Personal Identification Information**: We do not require you to create an account, register, or provide your name, email address, phone number, postal address, or any other identifying information.
- **No Location Data**: We do not request, access, or track your GPS or network-based physical location.
- **No Device Identifiers**: We do not collect your IMEI, MAC address, Advertising ID (IDFA/GAID), or hardware serial numbers.
- **No Contacts or Media Access**: The app does not access your address book, photos, camera, microphone, or storage files outside its private app directory.
- **No Financial Data**: The app is free to use and does not process any payment or financial information.

---

## 3. Data Stored Locally On Your Device

To provide a convenient worship experience, the app stores a minimal set of user preferences **locally on your device** using private application storage (`AsyncStorage`):

- **Favorites**: List of hymn IDs you have marked as favorites.
- **Custom Service Setlists**: Names and hymn selections of personal service orders you create (e.g., Sunday Divine Service, Vigils).
- **Recent Hymns**: A list of recently opened hymns to let you quickly jump back to hymns used during church service.
- **User Settings**: Your preferred reading theme (*Sacred Light*, *Night Navy*, *Sepia Parchment*) and your custom reading font size.

**Important:** This data resides strictly on your physical device. It is never uploaded to any remote server, cloud storage, or external database. If you uninstall the application or clear app data in your device settings, this locally stored data is permanently removed.

## 4. Device Permissions

The C&S Hymn Book app requires **zero sensitive or dangerous permissions**:
- No Internet permission is required to access hymn lyrics, liturgy, or search features.
- No Background location or wake locks.
- No Bluetooth, NFC, or biometric sensors.

## 5. Third-Party Services and Advertising

- **No Advertisements**: The application does not display third-party advertisements and contains no advertising SDKs (e.g., Google AdMob, Unity Ads, AppLovin).
- **No Analytics Trackers**: We do not integrate tracking or analytics libraries (e.g., Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Facebook Pixel) that monitor your usage or device behavior.
- **No Data Brokers**: We do not sell, rent, or trade any user information to any third party under any circumstances.

## 6. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR Compliance)

Our application is intended for general audiences, families, and worshipers of all ages, including children participating in church Sunday School and youth services. 

Because we do not collect any personal information whatsoever, our application fully complies with:
- The **Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)** of the United States.
- The **General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)** of the European Union.
- Relevant national child privacy standards worldwide.

## 7. Security of Content

All 980 hymns, prayers, and liturgical texts are bundled locally within the application package (`cshymns.json`). Because the app does not communicate with external servers during normal operation, your usage cannot be intercepted, monitored, or subjected to network-based security breaches.
