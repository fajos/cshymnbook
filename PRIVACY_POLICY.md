## 1. Introduction

Welcome to **C&S Hymn Book**, developed by **Fajostech / Femi Adeyemi** ("we", "our", or "us"). We are deeply committed to respecting and protecting the privacy of our users.

This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile and web applications handle user information. **In short: We do not collect, store, transmit, track, or share any of your personal data.** The app is designed for spiritual devotion, choir rehearsal, and congregational worship.

All core devotional content—including 980 hymns, Yoruba and English translations, liturgical orders, kneeling prayers (*Orin Akúnlẹkọ*), revival songs (*Orin Ìsọjí*), Psalms, and 350 curated Hymn Histories—is bundled locally and functions 100% offline without requiring internet access.

## 2. Information We Do Not Collect

When you use C&S Hymn Book, you remain completely anonymous:

- **No Personal Identification Information**: We do not require you to create an account, register, or provide your name, email address, phone number, postal address, or any identifying credentials.
- **No Location Data**: We do not request, access, monitor, or track your GPS or network-based physical location.
- **No Device Identifiers**: We do not collect your IMEI, MAC address, Advertising ID (IDFA/GAID), or hardware serial numbers.
- **No Contacts or Private Media Access**: The app does not access your address book, photos, camera, or microphone.
- **No Financial Data**: The application is free to use and contains no in-app purchases or payment processing mechanisms.
- **No Analytics or Behavioral Telemetry**: We do not monitor how you navigate, what hymns you read, or how long you use the application.

## 3. Data Stored Locally On Your Device

To provide a convenient worship and study experience, the app stores a minimal set of user preferences **strictly on your physical device** within private application storage:

- **Favorites**: List of hymn IDs you have bookmarked.
- **Custom Service Setlists**: Names and hymn selections of personal service orders you create (e.g., Sunday Divine Service, Harvest, Vigils, Choir Rehearsals).
- **Recent Hymns**: A list of recently opened hymns to let you quickly jump between hymns during church services.
- **User Settings**: Your preferred reading visual theme (*Sacred Light*, *Night Navy*, *Sepia Parchment*), custom reading font size, and musical preferences (playback speed, default instrument).
- **Offline Audio Tune Cache**: When you play or bulk-download hymn tunes (organ or piano accompaniments), the audio MP3 files are stored locally in the app's sandboxed document directory (`Paths.document/tunes/`). This enables gapless offline playback without re-downloading. You can clear this offline audio cache at any time within the app.

**Important:** This data resides strictly on your physical device. It is never uploaded to any remote server, cloud storage, or external database. If you uninstall the application or clear app data in your device settings, this locally stored data is permanently removed.

---

## 4. Network Connectivity & Audio CDN

- **Offline-First Architecture**: Hymn lyrics, categories, catechism, church seasons, liturgical orders, and hymn histories require **no internet connection** and are always accessible offline.
- **Optional Audio Playback**: If you choose to listen to musical accompaniments or download the offline audio pack, the app connects to our public Content Delivery Network (Cloudflare R2: `https://pub-b4f0c6d4ce79432b9b0d7d5417700a4b.r2.dev`) strictly via standard HTTP GET requests to retrieve static audio files.
- **Zero Logging or Telemetry**: No personal identification, user accounts, IP tracking logs, or listening habit profiles are gathered or stored by the developer during audio streaming or downloading.

## 5. Native System Sharing

When using the "Share Hymn Story" or lyric sharing features, the app invokes the operating system's native share sheet (`Share.share`). 

- The content is handed directly to the destination app you choose (such as Messages, WhatsApp, or Email).
- We do not intercept, record, monitor, or transmit your shared messages or recipient details to any server.
