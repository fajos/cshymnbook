About the Application

C&S Hymn Book is built to faithfully preserve and celebrate the divine heritage of the Eternal Sacred Order of the Cherubim & Seraphim Church worldwide.

980 Total Hymns Complete Yoruba & English translations, knelt-down prayers (Akúnlẹkọ), and revival songs (Ìsọjí).
