Summary: The C&S Hymn Book app operates 100% offline. We do not collect, transmit, store, track, or share any personal identity data, location, or telemetry from your device.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to the C&S Hymn Book mobile application (package name: com.fajostech.cshymnbook), published by Fajostech (“we”, “our”, or “us”). We respect your privacy and are committed to ensuring a completely safe, private worship environment for all users worldwide.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

When you use C&S Hymn Book, you remain entirely anonymous:

  • No Account Required: You do not need to register, log in, or provide any email address, phone number, name, or password.
  • No Location Data: The application does not access, monitor, or track your GPS or network location.
  • No Device Identifiers: We do not collect device IMEI, MAC address, Advertising IDs (GAID/IDFA), or serial numbers.
  • No Access to Private Files: The app does not access your photos, camera, contacts, microphone, or external storage.
  • No Payment Data: The app is free to use and does not process financial transactions.

3. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The app stores non-identifiable user preferences strictly inside your device’s internal application storage (AsyncStorage):

  • Favorites: Bookmarked hymn numbers for fast access.
  • Service Setlists: Custom collections you create for Sunday Divine Service, Harvest, Vigils, or Choir rehearsals.
  • Recent Hymns: List of recently viewed hymns to simplify navigation during church services.
  • Display Preferences: Your selected visual theme (Sacred Light, Night Navy, Sepia Parchment) and your preferred reading font size.

Important: This data never leaves your device. It is never transmitted to external cloud servers, and is erased if you uninstall the application or clear app data in your phone settings.

4. Permissions

The application requires zero sensitive device permissions. It works completely offline, eliminating risk of network eavesdropping or unauthorized data sharing.

5. Third-Party Services & Advertising

  • No Ads: The application contains no advertising SDKs or banner ads.
  • No Analytics or Tracking: The application does not contain behavioral analytics tools, telemetry trackers, or crash reporting spyware.
  • No Data Sales: We do not sell, rent, or trade any information to data brokers or third parties.

6. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & GDPR)

The application is designed for family, youth, and congregational worship. Because we collect zero personal data, C&S Hymn Book complies fully with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

7. Content & Offline Security

All 980 hymns, kneeling hymns (Orin Akúnlẹkọ), revival songs (Orin Ìsọjí), Psalms (Orin Dáfídì), and liturgical texts are bundled locally within the installation package (cshymns.json). You can worship anywhere without requiring an active cellular or Wi-Fi connection.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to maintain compliance with legal requirements or app store guidelines. Any revisions will be published on this page and in the GitHub repository.
